MIΛ is Study Space’s learning companion. It is designed to explain, prompt and practise with learners — not simply do their schoolwork for them.

M
MIΛ · Learning companionExample tutoring conversation
STUDENT I don't understand how to solve 3x + 5 = 20.
MIΛ Let's work through it. What could we do first to get the term with x on its own?
STUDENT Subtract 5 from both sides?
MIΛ Exactly. That gives 3x = 15. What operation would undo multiplying by 3?
A guided example · not a live chat or a promise of a particular answer
HOW MIΛ SUPPORTS LEARNING

  • Break difficult topics into smaller explanations.
  • Use questions and hints to encourage independent thinking.
  • Help learners revise, summarise and practise.

GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
get started.

Here are a few things students and families often want to know.

Who can use Study Space?＋

Study Space is intended for learners aged 9 and above. Students, parents and educators can explore the public website to understand the platform.

Does registration require a Student ID?＋

No Student ID is needed to begin public enrolment. Follow the registration steps in the student app. A Student ID is generated during the enrolment process.

What happens if a student is under 18?＋

The account must go through guardian verification before student access is approved. The Study Space team can assist with the in-person approval process.

Can I use Study Space on my phone?＋

Yes. Open the student app in a mobile browser. If your browser and device support installation, use its Install app or Add to Home Screen option. You can also continue using it online.

Is there a free trial?＋

New student accounts start with a 7-day free trial. Subscription details are available inside the student app.

How do I get help or ask a question?＋

Contact Study Space on WhatsApp at +268 7994 0955 or email ayandaagustozwane@gmail.com.

Does MIΛ do students’ schoolwork for them?＋

MIΛ is intended as a learning companion. It can help explain ideas and support study, but students should use it to understand their work and develop their own skills.

Which curricula and subjects are supported?

The student app currently offers Primary school, Secondary school, High school / Sixth form, College and University levels. Curriculum choices include National curriculum, IGCSE / Cambridge, A-Level, IB, CAPS and Other / Not sure. Subject catalogues vary by level and curriculum, and students can add a subject or course that is not listed. These profile options do not guarantee a complete set of learning resources for every syllabus. Contact us with the school, grade and subjects to confirm availability.

How much does a subscription cost?

Current plans are E100 for 7 days, E180 for 14 days and E350 for 28 days. New accounts receive a 7-day free trial. MTN MoMo payments are manually verified using the Student ID as the payment reference.

Can a school request a demonstration?

Yes. Teachers and school representatives can contact Study Space on WhatsApp to request a demo and discuss group enrolment or school pricing.

How does Study Space handle children’s information?

We collect account and learning information needed to provide the service. Under-18 accounts require guardian verification. Read our Privacy Policy for information about data use, AI services, rights and deletion requests.

Can a parent request account deletion?

Yes. A student or parent/guardian can contact Study Space through WhatsApp or email to request access, correction or deletion. We may need to verify the requester and may retain certain records where the law requires it.

Does Study Space send weekly progress reports to parents?

Parents can contact our team for help understanding the platform and learner support options. Automated weekly WhatsApp progress reports are not currently advertised as a standard feature.
