Privacy Policy

This notice explains how Study Space handles personal information when you visit our website or use the student and Monitor platforms.

Effective date: 13 November 2026 · Version 1.0

Important

Study Space is an education service based in Ezulwini, Eswatini. This policy is a plain-language service notice, not a legal opinion or certification of compliance. We are reviewing our processes against the Eswatini Data Protection Act, 2022 and applicable guidance. Please contact us if you need clarification.

1. Who is responsible?

Study Space, based in Ezulwini, Eswatini, is responsible for deciding why and how personal information is used for this service. Contact: ayandaagustozwane@gmail.com or WhatsApp +268 7994 0955.

2. Information we may collect

The information collected depends on the features used and may include:

  • Account and identity information: name or preferred name, date of birth, gender where requested, Student ID, login/account details.
  • Education information: school, grade or level, curriculum, selected subjects, learning plans, tasks, study sessions, practice results and progress.
  • Guardian information: parent or guardian contact and approval information needed to verify accounts for learners under 18.
  • Content you provide: uploaded notes and learning resources, messages, feedback and questions sent to MIΛ.
  • Payments: payment references and verification status. Manual mobile-money confirmation may involve the Student ID and payment details supplied to Study Space.
  • Technical information: basic device, browser, log and security information generated when using the apps or website.

Please do not send passwords, identity documents or highly sensitive personal details through public review forms or ordinary WhatsApp messages unless Study Space specifically requests information through an appropriate process.

3. Why we use information

  • Create and maintain student and Monitor accounts.
  • Verify guardian approval for learners under 18 and support account safety.
  • Provide study planning, learning resources, assignments, communication and progress features.
  • Respond to support requests, investigate errors and protect the service.
  • Verify subscriptions and payments.
  • Improve the educational service and meet legal obligations.
  • Publish testimonials or images only where the necessary separate permission has been obtained.

4. Children and guardian verification

Study Space is intended for learners aged 9 and above. For a learner under 18, the account remains subject to guardian verification before access is approved. The guardian approval process may involve collecting guardian details and recording the approval. A guardian does not need their own smartphone for the in-person approval process currently offered.

We aim to use children’s information only for the educational, account, safety and support purposes explained here. A parent or guardian can contact us to ask about a child’s information, request correction or raise a concern.

5. MIΛ and AI learning support

MIΛ is an AI-powered study companion. When a learner sends a question, prompt, image or other content to an AI feature, that content may be processed by the AI model service configured for the feature, in addition to the systems that operate Study Space. AI service providers and their processing locations may change as the platform is maintained. Contact us for information about the providers currently configured.

MIΛ can make mistakes and is not a teacher, guardian, counsellor, medical professional or emergency service. Learners should not submit passwords, private family information or other sensitive information to MIΛ. Parents and teachers should guide younger learners and review important answers.

6. Service providers and international processing

Study Space uses third-party infrastructure to operate its website, database, storage and AI features. These providers may process information on servers outside Eswatini. The providers and locations depend on the service configuration. We are responsible for assessing provider terms, security, confidentiality and any safeguards required for cross-border transfers. Contact us for the current provider information relevant to your account.

7. Sharing information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be accessible to authorised Study Space personnel and relevant service providers where needed to operate, secure or support the service. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect users and the service. We do not publish a learner’s name, photograph, school or identifiable feedback as a testimonial without separate permission.

8. Retention and security

We keep information for as long as it is needed for the educational and account purposes described above, to resolve disputes, maintain security and meet legal or payment-record obligations. Retention periods depend on the type of record. We use access controls and technical and organisational safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access personal information, correct inaccurate information, object to certain processing or request deletion. A parent or guardian may contact us regarding a child’s information. We may ask for reasonable proof of identity or authority before acting. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate account, security or payment purposes.

10. How to request account deletion

  1. Contact Study Space using the WhatsApp number or email below.
  2. State that you are requesting account deletion and provide the Student ID or account details needed to locate the account. Do not send your password.
  3. If the request concerns a child, we may need to verify the parent or guardian’s authority.
  4. We will review the request, explain any information that must be retained and arrange deletion or de-identification of eligible records.

11. Website forms and cookies

The public website contact form opens a pre-filled email in your own email application. It does not submit information to a website database. The website does not currently advertise third-party behavioural advertising or analytics. Your browser may still store ordinary technical data or cache website files.

12. Changes and complaints

We may update this policy when the platform or legal requirements change. The current version and effective date will appear on this page. You may contact Study Space first with a privacy question or complaint. You may also contact the Eswatini Data Protection Authority (EDPA), whose mandate is administered by the Eswatini Communications Commission.
